About us

A Legacy Built on Faith & Courage

The name Uganda Martyrs University (UMU) carries profound historical meaning. It was established in October 1993 by the Uganda Episcopal Conference, born out of a strategic vision to rebuild and morally fortify Ugandan society through value-based education following years of national civil challenges. The institution honors the young 22 Catholic Uganda Martyrs who were executed at Namugongo in 1886 for their unwavering faith and conviction.Opening its gates with a modest pioneer class of just 84 students, the university achieved its full, autonomous Civil Charter on April 2, 2005, signed into effect under the guidance of the Uganda National Council for Higher Education (NCHE). Over the years, trailblazing church leaders like Cardinal Emmanuel Wamala championed its growth, a legacy commemorated by the university’s newly recommissioned, state-of-the-art Emmanuel Wamala Auditorium at the Main Campus in Nkozi.

Recent Milestones & Modern Evolution

Today, UMU has evolved into a powerhouse of research and educational inclusivity, boasting a student population of over 6,700 active learners spread across a network of regional campuses. The university has recently solidified its academic presence through major international wins and infrastructure updates:

Global Research Partnerships: UMU officially joined the prestigious TAGDev 2.0 (Transforming African Agricultural Universities) consortium in partnership with the Mastercard Foundation. This 10-year initiative (running through 2033) funds comprehensive scholarships for disadvantaged youth, women, and refugees pursuing agricultural innovation and climate-adaptive sciences. The 31st Graduation Milestone: The university hosted its landmark 31st Graduation Ceremony, themed “Innovation for Social Economic Transformation through Science, Technology, and Enterprise.”. The university awarded degrees to 2,104 graduates, including the decoration of nine advanced PhD recipients across various multi-disciplinary fields. Northern Expansion: The educational footprint has officially expanded further into Northern Uganda with the formal inauguration and structural development of the Ngetta Campus in Lira, steering professional tracks in agriculture, teaching, and commercial business education directly to the local community. Positioning for the 21st Century By actively synthesizing a strict moral compass with practical digital tools, UMU ensures its graduates are not merely receiving a certificate, but are stepping directly into a legacy of ethical leadership. Moving forward, the university remains dedicated to scaling its online virtual learning software, upgrading regional student centers, and expanding its specialized professional faculties.
